Install on your TV

  1. Get the file. Open the release page and pick the APK under Assets. On your TV use the Downloader app, or copy it over with a USB drive.
  2. Allow installs. In Settings › Apps › Special app access, allow unknown sources for the app you open the APK with.
  3. Install. Open the APK and choose Install, or run adb install Glass.apk from a computer.
  4. Set as home. Press Home and choose Glass Launcher when asked.
